# A notebook is a garden, not an archive

A journal records what happened. A notebook records what is useful now. I like having room for both.

Some notes start as a single question. Others become a recipe after a few rounds of testing. Keeping them editable makes it easier to return and leave the next reader a better explanation.

## Leave a trail

Revision history makes changes less precious. Improve the wording, replace an example, or write down what did not work. The earlier version still has a place.

Try it here: open a notebook page, choose **Edit**, and add an observation of your own.
