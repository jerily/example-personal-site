# Now

A small snapshot of what is on the workbench. Unlike a journal entry, this page can change whenever the work does.

## Building

A personal site that starts with one command. A little Tcl handles routing and lifecycle; two independent packages take care of the notebook and journal.

## Learning

How to keep application boundaries clear. The host owns connections and startup. The mounted packages own their tables, content rules, and editors.

## Practicing

Writing useful comments: explain why a connection belongs to a worker, rather than narrating every assignment.
